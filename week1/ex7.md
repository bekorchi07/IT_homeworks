  <h1>How to Build a Simple Wooden Shelf</h1>

  <h2>What You Need</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>2 wooden boards (60cm x 30cm)</li>
    <li>4 metal brackets</li>
    <li>Wood screws</li>
    <li>Electric drill</li>
    <li>Sandpaper</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Steps</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>Sand both wooden boards until the surface is smooth.</li>
    <li>Mark the positions for the brackets on the wall.</li>
    <li><strong>Warning: use a stud finder</strong> before drilling into the wall.</li>
    <li>Attach the brackets to the wall using the drill and screws.</li>
    <li>Place the <em>wooden board</em> on top of the brackets and secure it firmly.</li>
  </ol>
